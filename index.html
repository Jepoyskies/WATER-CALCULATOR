                        Backup & Transfer

                        📱 Moving between your laptop and phone
                        Enter the day on your phone, tap Export Backup, send yourself the file, then open this app on the laptop and tap Import. Nothing is ever overwritten — only newer copies win.

                        🛡️ Keep your data safe

                        Everything lives in a real database inside this browser. Clearing browser data will erase it, so export a backup regularly — especially before updating your browser or changing computers.

                        🔁 Automatic file backup off

                        Pick a file once and the app rewrites it every time you save, so a copy always exists outside the browser.
